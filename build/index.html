BREADBASEMENT

Small games for two people and one phone. Nothing to download, nothing to sign up for, nothing your school's wifi has to think very hard about.

🏈 Fantasy Draft Showdown Twenty bucks each, five roster slots, sealed bids. Real video-game ratings stay hidden until the gavel drops. 2 players or vs. computer · NFL / NBA / Football
Game 02Timing · 3 min
⏱️ Split Second One of you holds a button for as long as you like. The other has to hold it for exactly that long — no clock, no counting out loud. Closest gap wins. 2 players · pass and play
Game 03Words · 6 min
🥪 Short Order You get a secret word. Say how few words you need to describe it, then write the clue. Get understood in one word and you clean up. 2 players · pass and play

How this works

  1. Everything is hot-seat — one phone or laptop, handed back and forth. The screen tells you when to pass it.
  2. No accounts, no rooms, no codes. Nothing you type leaves the device.
  3. Every game works offline once the page has loaded, so a dead wifi bar mid-round won't kill you.
  4. Games are built to be put down. Any round can be the last one.
